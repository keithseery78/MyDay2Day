Tasks

Tasks are more than things to tick off; they are small promises you make to yourself. By capturing them in one calm place, you free your mind from constant remembering and worrying. Marking tasks as complete gives your brain a clear signal of progress, which can reduce stress and build a quiet sense of achievement. Even on difficult days, seeing a few small wins reminds you that you are still moving forward. Use this list gently: not as a pressure to do everything, but as a way to choose what matters most for your wellbeing today.

Calendar

The calendar is your long‑view of progress. Every dot is a day you showed up for yourself – by completing tasks, writing a journal entry or noting gratitude. Seeing these days together helps you notice patterns, not perfection, and reminds you how often you have kept going.

Tap a date to review what you did and wrote on that day, or use the filter to focus on tasks, completed work or reflections. Let this view encourage consistency over time, especially on days when your progress feels smaller than it really is.
